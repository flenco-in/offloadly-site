Privacy Policy

Offloadly · Last updated 11 October 2026
SupportPrivacy Policy

In short: Offloadly does not collect, store, sell or share any of your data. Your photos, videos and files never leave your devices.

What Offloadly accesses, and why

  • Photos library — only to copy the photos and videos you choose to your drive, show what is backed up, find similar photos, restore items from your drive, and, only when you ask, remove items that are verified on your drive.
  • Files and drives you pick — only the folders and drives you select, to copy files and read or tidy up your backups.
  • Face ID / Touch ID — only if you turn on App Lock. Apple handles the check; Offloadly never sees your biometric data.
  • Notifications — only if you turn on backup reminders. They are created on your iPhone.

What is stored, and where

On your iPhone, Offloadly keeps your settings, a list of past backups and a catalogue of which files are on which drive (file names, folder paths, sizes and fingerprints — never file contents). On your drive, it keeps the same kind of index and a plain-text report of each backup. All of this stays on your devices and is deleted when you delete the app or the files.

No tracking, no analytics, no ads

Offloadly contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code and makes no network connections for backups. It has no account and no server.

Tips

Optional tips are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. Offloadly receives no payment or personal details.

Children

Offloadly collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
